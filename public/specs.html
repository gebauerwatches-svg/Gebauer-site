The numbers that matter. Ask me for anything not here.

Edition300 total, 100 of each wood, numbered within its wood
AssemblyJapan. Packaging made in northern Italy

5 ATM covers rain, handwashing and a shower. It is not a swimming or diving rating.

The three woods

Cherry — $299Starts pale, darkens to a deep reddish brown within months
Black Ebony — $339Dense and nearly black. Effectively permanent
African Padauk — $375Fiery orange, deepening toward burgundy over years

Three woods, three speeds. A sun-exposure test on bare and oiled samples of each is running now to measure how fast.

The caseback

Each watch carries its own number within its wood, so a padauk reads PADAUK 042 / 100 rather than a single count across all 300.
