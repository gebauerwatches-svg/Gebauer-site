Specifications.

Everything we have confirmed, and nothing we haven't.

Movement

CalibreSeiko VH31
TypeMeca-quartz
Seconds handSweeping, not ticking
OriginJapan

Meca-quartz pairs quartz accuracy with the sweeping seconds-hand motion of a mechanical movement. It is the reason the watch reads as mechanical on the wrist without a mechanical movement's price or service schedule.

Case and crystal

Diameter39.0mm
Lug to lug44.0mm
Thickness10.0mm
Dial opening31.0mm
Crown6.0mm
Case material316L stainless steel
CrystalSapphire, flat (not domed), anti-reflective coated
Water resistance5 ATM
CasebackChemical-etched raven, wordmark, and edition number

5 ATM means rain, handwashing and a shower. It is not a swimming or diving rating.

Dial and hands

DialReal wood, cut individually
HandsDauphine, luminescent
Wood optionsCherry, Black Ebony, African Padauk

No two dials share a grain pattern, so every watch is one of one before the numbering is applied.

MaterialStainless steel

The three woods

WoodHow it ages
Cherry — $299Starts pale, darkens to a deep reddish brown. Most of the change happens within the first months.
Black Ebony — $339Dense and nearly black. Effectively permanent.
African Padauk — $375Starts a fiery orange and deepens toward burgundy over years.

Three woods, three speeds: one changes in months, one over years, one barely at all. A sun-exposure test on bare and oiled samples of each is running now to measure exactly how fast.

The caseback

Chemical etched, 30mm. The number is the watch's own place in its wood's run of 100, so each variant has its own sequence rather than one count across all 300.
