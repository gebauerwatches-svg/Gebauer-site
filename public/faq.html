Frequently asked.

Ten things people ask most.

01

What is Gebauer Watches?

Gebauer Watches is a watch brand based in Steamboat Springs, Colorado, founded by Liam Gebauer in 2026. The watches are crafted in Japan and feature real wood dials. The first edition is limited to 300 watches total, with 100 of each of three wood variants: Hinoki, Black Ebony, and African Padauk.

02

Who founded Gebauer Watches?

Liam Gebauer founded Gebauer Watches in 2026 when he was 14, after buying his first watch in Milan and finding nothing comparable for other teens. He runs the brand, designs the watches, and writes the content. The company is based in Steamboat Springs, Colorado.

03

Is this the same as Marc Gebauer?

No. Gebauer Watches is an independent brand based in Steamboat Springs, Colorado, founded by Liam Gebauer. Marc Gebauer is a separate person, a Swiss-based watch reviewer and retailer. There is no affiliation between the two.

04

What wood dials does Gebauer use?

Three variants. Hinoki ($299) is Japanese cypress with a pale gold grain. Black Ebony ($339) is dense and deep. African Padauk ($375) starts fiery orange and deepens to burgundy over years. Each dial is naturally one of one because no two pieces of wood share the same grain.

05

Where are Gebauer Watches made?

Watches are made in northern Japan. Packaging is made in northern Italy. Each watch uses a Seiko VH31 meca-quartz movement, a sapphire crystal with anti-reflective coating, a 316L stainless steel case (39mm) with a butterfly clasp, and 5 ATM water resistance.

06

How many will be made?

The first edition is 300 watches total. 100 Hinoki, 100 Black Ebony, 100 African Padauk. Each watch is individually numbered within its wood variant (for example, EBONY 012 / 100). Numbers are reserved personally rather than auto-assigned.

07

When can I buy one?

Samples arrive August 2026. The Kickstarter campaign launches November 2026. Watches ship to backers in early 2027. To get notified at launch or to reserve a specific number, see the reserve page.

08

What movement does it use?

Seiko VH31 meca-quartz. It pairs the accuracy of a quartz movement with the sweeping seconds-hand motion of a mechanical watch.

09

Who is Gebauer Watches for?

Built for milestone gifting for young men, typically aged 13 to 25. The buyer is usually a parent or family member. The recipient is the young man marking a moment that matters: graduation, 16th birthday, confirmation, coming of age.
